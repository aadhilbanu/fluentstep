Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and a securely hashed password (we never store your actual password).
  • Learning data: the text of your practice answers, journal entries, conversations, translation attempts, saved words, mistakes, quiz scores and practice time.
  • Settings: your level, daily plan, reminder time and language preferences.

What we do not collect

  • Voice recordings are never uploaded or stored. Recording and playback happen only in your browser.
  • We do not use advertising trackers and we do not sell your data.

Services that process your text

  • Speech-to-text: your browser (Chrome or Edge) may send your voice to the browser maker's speech service to turn it into text.
  • Grammar checking: the text of each answer you submit for feedback is sent to LanguageTool (their privacy policy).
  • AI coach (only if enabled): if the AI coach is turned on, your answers are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate feedback (Anthropic privacy policy).
  • Hosting and database: the app and your learning data are stored with our hosting and database providers.

Why we use your data

Only to run FluentStep for you: to give feedback, save your progress, show your reports and keep your account secure.

Your choices and rights

  • You can delete your account and all of your learning data at any time from Settings → Delete my account. This is permanent.
  • You can remove items from your mistake notebook and vocabulary list at any time.
  • For any privacy question, contact the FluentStep administrator using the contact details on the Terms page.

Children

FluentStep is intended for college students and adults. If you are under 18, please use it with a parent's or guardian's permission.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated.
